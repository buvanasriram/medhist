# Welcome to medHist: Your Family's Health Story

## 🏥 What is medHist?
medHist is a private, secure medical timeline manager designed for families. Most of us have medical records scattered across emails, paper folders, and hospital portals. medHist uses **Advanced AI** to help you organize these into one clear, searchable clinical history.

---

## 🚀 The Core Magic
1. **AI Extraction**: Just snap a photo or upload a PDF of a blood test, prescription, or scan. Our AI automatically extracts the date, the doctor's name, a simple summary, and even upcoming appointments.
2. **Family Folders**: Create profiles for your children, spouse, or elderly parents. Each person has their own private timeline.
3. **Medication Manager**: No more forgetting dosages. medHist tracks active medications and can even explain the purpose of a pill if the report is unclear.
4. **AI Health Assistant**: Ask questions like "When was Sam's last blood test?" or "What did the doctor say about Dad's heart?" and get instant answers with links to the original reports.

---

## 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 User Journeys (How to use the app)

### 1. The Busy Parent
*   **The Scenario**: Your child has a recurring allergy. You have 3 different reports from 2 different doctors over the last year.
*   **The Journey**: You upload all three reports to the child's profile. You use the **Search Bar** to type "Allergy." Instantly, you see the trend of how the symptoms changed. Before the next specialist visit, you hit **"Download Brief"** to get a 1-page summary to hand to the doctor.

### 2. The Caretaker (Managing Parents)
*   **The Scenario**: You are helping manage your elderly father's health, but your siblings also help out.
*   **The Journey**: You create a Family Group and send your siblings the **Invite Code**. Now, when you take Dad to the cardiologist, you upload the report. Your sister, who lives in another city, gets an alert on her dashboard about Dad's **upcoming follow-up appointment** in two weeks.

### 3. The Chronic Patient
*   **The Scenario**: You take 5 different medications and often forget which doctor prescribed what.
*   **The Journey**: You check your **Medication Manager** on your phone. You see exactly when you started each med. You use the **AI Assistant** to ask: "Are there any notes about my blood pressure from last month?" The AI finds the specific report and highlights the doctor's advice.

### 4. The "Prepared Traveler"
*   **The Scenario**: You are on vacation and need to visit an urgent care clinic. They ask for your medical history.
*   **The Journey**: You open medHist on your phone (it's installed as a PWA). You find the relevant report, click **"Share"**, and generate a secure 24-hour link. You show the QR code or send the link to the doctor. They now have your full history instantly.

## 📱 Getting Started for Testers
1. **Sign Up**: Create an account and "Create a New Family."
2. **Install it**: On your phone, look for the **"Install App"** button or use "Add to Home Screen" in your browser menu. This makes medHist feel like a real app!
3. **Upload a Test File**: Don't have a real report handy? Use a receipt or any document with a date and some text to see how the AI handles it.
4. **Try the Chat**: Tap the blue stethoscope icon in the bottom right to ask the Assistant questions about what you uploaded.
